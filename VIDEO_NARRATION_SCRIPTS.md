# Recording Instructions

## What You Need
1. **OBS Studio** (free) or **Windows Game Bar** (Win+G) for screen recording
2. **ElevenLabs.io** (free tier) for voice cloning + narration audio
3. **CapCut** (free) or **DaVinci Resolve** (free) for combining video + audio

## Steps
1. Go to [elevenlabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io) → Sign up → Voice Lab → "Add Voice" → "Instant Voice Clone"
2. Record yourself reading 30-60 seconds of any script above (use your phone or laptop mic)
3. Upload the recording → Name your voice → Save
4. Go to "Speech Synthesis" → Select your cloned voice → Paste each script → Download audio
5. Open the app feature → Start OBS recording → Walk through the feature slowly and cleanly
6. Import screen recording + audio into CapCut → Align audio to video → Export as MP4

## Recording Tips
- Use 1920x1080 resolution
- Close all unnecessary apps and notifications
- Use dark mode — it looks more professional on recordings
- Move your mouse slowly and deliberately
- Pause for 1-2 seconds after each action so viewers can see what happened
- Keep the cursor visible and near the action area
- Record each feature separately — easier to edit
